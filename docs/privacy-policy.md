# Hush — Privacy Policy

_Effective 2026-09-16 · applies to Hush (`dev.tapio.hush`) for Android._
_Intended URL: https://tapio.dev/hush/privacy — the Play listing links here._

**Hush collects no personal data.** It has no network permission, so nothing
you do in the app can leave your device unless you send it yourself.

## What stays on your device
- **Your settings** — the sounds in your mix and their volumes, the master
  volume, the sleep-timer choice and the fade length. They live in the app's
  private storage so the app opens the way you left it. If Android backup is
  enabled on your device, Android may include these settings in your Google
  backup or a device-to-device transfer, under Google's terms; Hush itself
  never uploads them.
- **A crash report, only if Hush crashes** — a plain-text file with the time,
  the app version, your device model and Android version, and the technical
  stack trace. Hush tells you it exists and offers **Share report**; the report
  leaves the device only if you choose to share it, through whichever app you
  pick in the Android share sheet. **Dismiss** deletes it. It contains no
  account, contact, location or advertising identifiers.

## Permissions, and why
- **Notifications** — the playback notification with its pause and stop
  controls.
- **Foreground service (media playback)** — keeps the sound playing with the
  screen off and the app in the background.
- **Prevent the phone from sleeping** — so overnight playback is not paused by
  deep sleep.

Hush asks for nothing else: no location, contacts, microphone, camera, storage
or internet.

## Third parties
None. No analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service, no third-party
SDK that collects data. Every sound is synthesized on the device as it plays;
nothing is downloaded or streamed.

## Children
Hush is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It
collects no data from anyone.
